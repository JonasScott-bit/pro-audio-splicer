Output · loudness & ceiling

Loudness target — full-song convert
True-peak ceiling — every export

Every export is finished by a true-peak limiter at the ceiling: loudness is reached by limiting, never by raw gain past full scale, and the finished file is re-measured — clipped samples out: zero. Platforms normalize at playback, so a louder master gets turned down there; loudness buys density, not playback level. This is the pro delivery standard — no clipping — not a playback-volume promise.

6Library & sample pack

0 samples saved

The pack is one zip: a named top folder, one sub-folder per sound type (Kicks & Low, Hats & Perc, One-Shots, Tones & Pads, Loops & Texture, FX Performances), each file inside — in the export format picked at the top of the page — carrying your artist + product tags, ready to drop straight into any other app's sample library. “Download pack” saves the zip to Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android); “Save pack to Files / Drive…” opens your phone's share sheet so you can put the zip straight into Files or Google Drive. Each library item below also has its own “Files / Drive…” button for saving one sample at a time.

Nothing saved yet. Edit a slice, run it through the FX board and master, then “Save slice to library”.
Everything is processed locally in your browser — your audio never leaves this device. Every export carries the title, artist, album/product, genre, year and comment from the Track info card, in the tag format each file type reads best: WAV (16/24-bit, LIST/INFO + BWF loudness), MP3 (ID3v2), FLAC and OGG Opus (Vorbis-style comments), AIFF (text chunks + ID3), and M4A/AAC on devices whose own encoder offers it. All formats are encoded on this device, after the true-peak ceiling is applied, so encoder overshoot can't clip them.
